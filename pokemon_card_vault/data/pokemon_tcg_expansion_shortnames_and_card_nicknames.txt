POKÉMON TCG EXPANSION SHORTNAMES, SET CODES & CARD NICKNAMES
Research/reference file for Pokoin
Last checked online: 2026-09-12

IMPORTANT
- Keep canonical set codes separate from informal collector aliases.
- Prefer the canonical code for database identity and aliases only for search.
- Some short aliases collide heavily (BS, AR, DR, PF, SV, SS, TM, etc.).
- Card nicknames are community terms, not official Pokémon names.
- Informal nicknames evolve; this file focuses on high-confidence/common collector usage.

7) HIGH-RISK ALIAS COLLISIONS FOR SEARCH
======================================================================
BS | Base Set; informally can also mean Battle Styles, Brilliant Stars, or Burning Shadows
SV | Supreme Victors official code; also universal shorthand for Scarlet & Violet
SS | EX Sandstorm official code; also an informal shorthand for several S-starting sets
AR | Arceus official code; also commonly typed for Astral Radiance
DR | EX Dragon official code; can be typed for Destined Rivals or Delta Reign
TM | Triumphant official code; can also mean Twilight Masquerade
PF | informal shorthand can mean Paldean Fates or Phantasmal Flames
PE | informal shorthand can mean Paldea Evolved or Prismatic Evolutions
CR | informal shorthand can mean Chilling Reign or Chaos Rising
SSP | Surging Sparks expansion code; also SWSH promo-series notation in some databases
EX | Expedition Base Set official abbreviation; also the name of the 2003–2007 EX era/mechanic

Recommended Pokoin search model:
1. Exact canonical code + market + era is strongest.
2. Full expansion name is next.
3. Common aliases are non-unique search synonyms.
4. Card nicknames should resolve to an exact card/set/number where possible.
5. Never use a 2-letter collector alias as a database primary key.
