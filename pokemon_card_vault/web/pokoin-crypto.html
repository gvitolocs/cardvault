Pokoin Card Reserve

Pokoin is the marketplace layer for Pokémon card collectors. It combines Card Reserve inventory, seller listings, cart and watchlist flows, reserve analytics, PKN wallet rails and Pokoin Scan infrastructure.

What is Pokoin?

Pokoin combines a card marketplace, real catalog data, user-created seller listings, listing-aware carts, wallet-linked accounts and a dedicated marketplace signal page for reserve analytics.

PKN and settlement rails

PKN and wPKN remain settlement infrastructure for Pokoin. The current product focus is Card Reserve marketplace UX, seller listing quality, checkout data, watchlists and transparent reserve signals.
