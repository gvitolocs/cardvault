Pokoin Official Website

Pokoin is the official PKN Card Reserve marketplace and crypto collector platform. It combines Pokémon card inventory, seller listings, cart and watchlist flows, reserve analytics, PKN wallet rails, wPKN liquidity, native Pokoin NFTs and Pokoin Scan infrastructure.

What is Pokoin?

Pokoin combines a card marketplace, real catalog data, user-created seller listings, listing-aware carts, wallet-linked accounts and a dedicated marketplace signal page for reserve analytics. If you search Google for Pokoin, Pokoin crypto, PKN, wPKN, Card Reserve or Pokoin marketplace, this page points to the canonical official routes.

PKN and settlement rails

PKN and wPKN remain settlement infrastructure for Pokoin. The current product focus is Card Reserve marketplace UX, seller listing quality, checkout data, watchlists and transparent reserve signals.
