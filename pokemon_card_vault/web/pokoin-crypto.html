Pokoin Crypto (PKN)

Pokoin crypto is the ecosystem behind PKN, wPKN, PokoinPoS and CardVault. It is designed for Pokémon card collectors who want blockchain payments, transparent settlement and open DeFi liquidity.

What is Pokoin?

Pokoin combines a native Proof-of-Stake blockchain called PokoinPoS, the PKN coin, wrapped wPKN on BNB Chain, and CardVault, a crypto-native Pokémon card marketplace.

PKN and wPKN

PKN is the native currency of PokoinPoS. wPKN is the wrapped BEP-20 token on BNB Chain, backed by native PKN reserve records and visible through the public reserve manifest.
