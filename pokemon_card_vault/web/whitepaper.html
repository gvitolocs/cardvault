Pokoin Whitepaper v1.0

Collector Commerce, PKN Wallets, wPKN Liquidity And Marketplace Signals

Pokoin is a collector-first ecosystem combining Card Reserve marketplace tools, PKN wallet features, wPKN liquidity references, Pokoin Scan, marketplace signals and NFTs.

01

Purpose

Pokoin connects collectible commerce with transparent wallet and network tools. Card Reserve helps collectors browse Pokemon card inventory, compare seller listings, follow marketplace signals and use PKN wallet features.

02

Ecosystem

The Pokoin ecosystem includes Card Reserve marketplace pages, Pokoin accounts, PKN wallet flows, wPKN liquidity references, Pokoin Scan, marketplace signal analytics, NFT infrastructure, public documentation and support pages.

03

PKN And PokoinPoS

PKN is the native unit used by PokoinPoS network tooling and account-linked marketplace flows. Pokoin Scan exposes public RPC, health, supply, account, transaction and block information.

04

wPKN Liquidity

wPKN is the wrapped BNB Chain representation used for external liquidity references. Public reserve, token contract and PancakeSwap links help users verify wrapped-market visibility.

05

Card Reserve Marketplace

Card Reserve focuses on catalog imagery, card detail pages, seller listings, watchlists, cart flows, account profiles and card-specific share previews.

06

Marketplace Signals

Marketplace signal pages turn inventory, listing, price, rarity and demand context into readable signals. They are product information, not investment advice.

07

Trust And Safety

Pokoin avoids adult content, hate speech, graphic violence, harassment and misleading financial promises. Support will never ask for seed phrases, private keys, passwords or full payment card details.

08

Roadmap

The roadmap focuses on stronger card pages, better search and preview quality, clearer analytics, improved documentation, Pokoin Scan visibility and consistent ecosystem content.
